# Acrylic pet portraits with shipping across Colombia

Sac_Artx is a studio in Bogotá, Colombia, specialized in custom pet portraits hand-painted in acrylic. Orders ship to any city in Colombia.

## What it is

Sac_Artx hand-paints pet portraits in acrylic on canvas or on an acrylic (methacrylate) base, commissioned from a photograph. The studio also offers acrylic bases, personalized phone cases, and landscapes. Website: https://sacartx.art/en/

## Who it is for

People in Colombia who want an artist to paint their dog, cat, or other pet and deliver the piece to their city. There is no need to visit the studio: commissions and shipping are remote.

## What is included

- Hand-painted acrylic portrait.
- Digital sketch beforehand and free revisions.
- Secure packaging.
- Shipping across Colombia with a tracking number.
- Shipping outside Colombia is not available for now.

## Prices

Prices current as of October 2026, in Colombian pesos (COP). Shipping is extra. Canvas portraits from COP 120,000 (12×18 cm, 1 pet). Acrylic bases from COP 130,000. Pet phone case COP 100,000. Full list: https://sacartx.art/info/prices.md

## Turnaround and payment

Turnaround is 15 to 20 business days from the first 50% payment. The remaining 50% is paid when the work is finished. The piece ships once payment is complete. Payment methods: Nequi, Daviplata, bank transfer, and cash.
