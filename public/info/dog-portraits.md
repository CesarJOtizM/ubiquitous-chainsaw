# Hand-painted acrylic dog portraits in Colombia

Sac_Artx is a studio in Colombia, specializing in custom dog portraits hand-painted in acrylic. Each portrait is commissioned from a photograph sent on WhatsApp.

## What it is

Sac_Artx hand-paints your dog in acrylic on canvas or on an acrylic (methacrylate) base. A commission starts with a photo. A digital sketch is sent before painting, and revisions are free. Instagram and TikTok: @sac_artx. Website: https://sacartx.art/en/

## Who it is for

Dog owners in Colombia who want a custom painting, a gift, or a memorial portrait. Any breed or mixed breed is welcome. Several pets can share one painting; the suggested number depends on the size.

## What is included

- Hand-painted acrylic dog portrait on canvas or acrylic base.
- Digital sketch before painting.
- Sketch revisions at no extra charge.
- Final approval photo before shipping.
- Protective packaging and a tracking number within Colombia.

## Turnaround and payment

Turnaround is 15 to 20 business days from the first 50% payment. The remaining 50% is paid when the work is finished. The piece ships once payment is complete. Payment methods: Nequi, Daviplata, bank transfer, and cash.
