# How to commission a pet portrait with Sac_Artx

Sac_Artx is a studio in Colombia, specializing in custom pet portraits hand-painted in acrylic. Commissions are placed on WhatsApp, from a photograph.

## What it is

The Sac_Artx process has five steps: send the photo, receive a digital sketch, approve the design, paint the piece, and receive the shipment. Website: https://sacartx.art/en/

## Who it is for

Anyone commissioning a dog, cat, or other pet portrait, an acrylic base, or a personalized phone case, with follow-up on WhatsApp and Instagram.

## What is included

1. Send your photo: you send the best photo of your pet on WhatsApp or Instagram. Sac_Artx helps you choose the best shot.
2. Sketch within 48 hours: you receive a digital preview of the result.
3. Approve the design: whatever is needed gets adjusted, with no extra charge for revisions.
4. Painting: when the piece is ready, you receive photos on WhatsApp for final approval.
5. Delivery: secure packaging and shipping to Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla, and the rest of Colombia, with tracking.

The image can also be improved with artificial intelligence before painting: fixing the lighting or combining several photos.

## Prices

Acrylic portraits on canvas start at COP 120,000 (12×18 cm, 1 pet). Full prices: https://sacartx.art/info/prices.md. Catalog: https://sacartx.art/en/catalog/

## Turnaround and payment

Turnaround is 15 to 20 business days from the first 50% payment. The remaining 50% is paid when the work is finished. The piece ships once payment is complete. Payment methods: Nequi, Daviplata, bank transfer, and cash.
