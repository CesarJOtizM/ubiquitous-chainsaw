# Sac_Artx pet portrait prices in Colombia

Sac_Artx is a studio in Bogotá, Colombia, specialized in custom pet portraits hand-painted in acrylic. The prices below are current as of October 2026, in Colombian pesos (COP). Shipping is extra.

## What it is

Sac_Artx offers acrylic portraits on canvas, acrylic bases with a keychain, personalized phone cases, and commissioned landscapes. Each piece is hand-painted from a photograph. Website: https://sacartx.art/en/

## Who it is for

Anyone pricing a dog, cat, or other pet portrait, an acrylic base, a personalized phone case, or a landscape, with shipping inside Colombia.

## What is included

Each portrait or base price includes 1 pet, a digital sketch beforehand, and free revisions. National shipping is charged separately.
