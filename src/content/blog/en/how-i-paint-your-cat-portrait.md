I paint your cat by hand, in acrylic, from the photo you send me on WhatsApp. Short or long fur both work. The eyes, the whiskers, and the markings come from that photo, not from a generic breed. The smallest canvas (12×18 cm, 1 cat) is COP 120,000. I ship cash on delivery to Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla, and the rest of Colombia.

## How do I paint a cat’s markings and whiskers?

A photo from high above flattens the face. I need the eyes, the ears, and the whiskers in daylight. If the cat is calico, like Amelia, the black, the orange, and the white have to show in the shot: those patches cannot be guessed. On Amelia’s base the portrait sits on a blue heart and the name is on the wood.

If the cat moves and the photo is blurry, send another. Eye color and the pattern of the fur come from that shot. A second, closer photo is enough when the first one stays on the room instead of the face.

## Does an old photo work if the cat is gone?

Yes. I use the photos you still have, even if they are not recent. The process does not change: a WhatsApp sketch before I paint, free changes to that sketch, and shipping across Colombia. If you want the name on the piece, the base carries it on the wood, as on Amelia’s.
