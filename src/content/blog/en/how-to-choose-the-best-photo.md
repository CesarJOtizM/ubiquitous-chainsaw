The best photo for your pet portrait is a **sharp image, shot in natural light, and taken at your pet's eye level**. The better the photo, the more faithful and detailed the hand-painted acrylic portrait will be. Here is a clear guide to choosing it well before you place your order.

## What makes a photo good for a portrait?

A good reference photo gets three things right: **focus, light, and expression**. Focus lets us see the fur, eyes, and texture; light defines the true colors; and expression captures your pet's personality. When a photo has all three, the portrait starts from a solid base.

Avoid blurry, very dark, or far-away shots. Those lose exactly the details that bring a portrait to life.

## What should the lighting be like?

Use **natural light whenever you can**. A large window or a shaded patio during the day gives soft, even light that shows the true colors of the fur. Avoid direct flash: it flattens features and creates red reflections in the eyes. Skip harsh midday sun too, since it casts hard shadows.

## From what angle should I take the photo?

Get **down to your pet's eye level** instead of standing and shooting downward. Coming down to their level captures the gaze head-on and makes the portrait feel close and personal. For dogs and cats, a front or three-quarter view usually works better than a full profile.

## What resolution do I need?

Send the photo **at the highest quality possible, with no cropping and no filters**. A picture from a recent phone is more than enough, as long as it is the original file. Social-media filters change the colors and fur texture, so send us the unedited version.

## What if I don't have a perfect photo?

Don't worry: we work with the photo you have. Before accepting the commission we review the image and honestly tell you whether the quality might affect the result. We can also **improve the lighting or combine several photos** to build the best possible reference. And if you have lost your companion, we also paint memorial portraits from the photos you kept.

## How does the process work after I send the photo?

The process is simple, with constant communication:

1. You send the photo on WhatsApp.
2. We send a **digital sketch** for approval before painting, with free revisions.
3. We hand-paint your piece in acrylic on canvas or on an acrylic base.
4. We ship with safe packaging and tracking across Colombia.
