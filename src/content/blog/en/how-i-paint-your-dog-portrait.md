I paint your dog by hand, in acrylic, from the photo you send me on WhatsApp. Any breed or mix works, one dog or several on the same piece. The smallest canvas (12×18 cm, 1 dog) is COP 120,000. I ship cash on delivery to Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla, and the rest of Colombia.

## What does a dog portrait look like on canvas and on a base?

On canvas, the acrylic sits on cloth. On a base, the portrait sits on methacrylate with resin, a glass effect, and the name on the wood. These are finished pieces from the Sac_Artx catalog, not a mockup.

## What photo do I need so the muzzle and ears come out right?

The photo has to show the eyes, the muzzle, and the shape of the ears, in daylight, with no flash straight into the eyes. If the dog is far away or the shot is blurry, I cannot reach the coat or the expression. Move closer. If the first shot loses the face, send a second one.

If two dogs share the piece, I need each face. They do not have to be in the same photo: I join separate shots. That is how Mia and Azul share a base, and how Fionita’s two dogs share another. On Fionita’s base, one dog has angel wings.

The photo guide, with examples of what to avoid, is [what photo do I need to paint my pet](/en/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-photo/).
